## Transcription (handwritten parish baptism entry, Latin)

N. 251

Anno Domini Millesimo noncentesimo et decimoquinto

Die quinta Mensis Decembris

Ego Sac. [?] de licentio [?]

Rev.mi Blasio Palermo Archipresbyteri et Parochi

baptizavi infantem die 27 Octobris 1915 nato ex legitimis

conjugibus (ut ass.) Egidio Emmi

et Concetta Sgroi cui impositum

est nomen Gregorium

Matrina fuit Rosaria Emmi : [?]

vero Josepha Nicotra

## English Translation

N. 251

In the year of Our Lord one thousand nine hundred fifteen

On the fifth day of the month of December

I, Priest [?], by permission of [?]

Most Reverend Blasio Palermo, Archpriest and Parish Priest

I baptized an infant born on 27 October 1915 from legitimate

spouses (as assumed) Egidio Emmi

and Concetta Sgroi upon whom was imposed

the name Gregorio

Godmother was Rosaria Emmi [?]

Truly Josepha Nicotra
